## PRO-LOGIC Studio

Run the live tools of [PRO-LOGIC Studio](https://pro-logic.studio) from a Stream Deck: Live Graphics, Stage Timer, Presenter PRO, Teleprompter PRO, Take Board PRO and Intercom PRO, from one connection.

### Setting up

1. In PRO-LOGIC open any of those consoles, find the **Stream Deck / Companion** box and press **Show token and key**.
2. Paste the **Output token** and the **Control key** into this connection. The same pair runs every console of the account. The key runs your outputs: keep it private.
3. **App URL**: `https://pro-logic.studio`, or `http://localhost:3000` when PRO-LOGIC runs on the show computer (the keys then work with no internet).
4. Open **Presets** and drag keys onto your deck. They come in the Broadcast look (set **Look of the preset keys** to *Plain text* for text keys).

### What the keys do

- **Live Graphics**: graphics on / off, full screens, team flags, player plays and lower thirds, TAKE, CLEAR, score display. Red while on air.
- **Stage Timer**: time display (green, amber, red), start / pause, stop, restart, ±1 minute, next / previous cue, GO cue 1–8.
- **Presenter**: next, back, blank, on screen now, up next.
- **Teleprompter**: play / pause, faster, slower, top, back, ahead, speed display.
- **Take Board**: CLAP, ROLL / CUT, GOOD, NG, HOLD, take ±1, new shot, new scene, floor messages, slate display.
- **Intercom** (the director's beltpack in the Intercom console): TALK and LISTEN on channels 1–6, LATCH, MUTE, CALL ALL, status. TALK is held while the key is down; if the deck stops sending, the beltpack closes the microphone by itself after 2.5 seconds.

The connection settings choose what the deck follows (Presenter, Teleprompter, Take Board, Intercom) and how often it reads the state.
